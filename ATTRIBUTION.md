# Developer attribution

**Acadia Conference Management System (ACMS) was originally developed by Teng Teng.**

Developer: https://github.com/doubleteng  
Source: https://github.com/doubleteng/acadia-conference-management-system

The standard ACMS distribution automatically includes developer credit in:

- Author Portal, Conference Program and Recognition Portal page footers;
- the optional Program guide, including its standalone view;
- the offline configurator, Visual Editor, local demo and generated website embed snippets;
- plain-text and HTML presentation reminder messages;
- managed calendar and event descriptions;
- newly generated document templates.

The displayed credit is **“Developed by Teng Teng.”** It is separate from conference edition settings, so changing the year, branding, venues or organizer contacts does not replace the developer attribution. Deploying the generated files to another Google account retains it. Annual conference organizers remain responsible for their own content and operation; developer attribution does not imply endorsement of an edition's decisions.

The source copyright and MIT license identify Teng Teng. MIT requires the copyright and permission notice to accompany copies or substantial portions of the software; it does not itself impose a separate mandatory visual-credit clause. The standard source implements visible credit automatically. As with other open-source software, someone who modifies the source can alter its presentation; this is not a tamper-proof attribution mechanism.
